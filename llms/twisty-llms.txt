# Twisty

> Twisty is an AI character creation platform from Bright Live Inc. (dba Twisty) that lets creators generate lifelike AI characters and personalized, expressive video messages. It is a consumer/creator product available on the web and via iOS and Android apps, with a free tier and a paid monthly plan. Twisty does not currently publish a public API, developer documentation, or SDKs.
